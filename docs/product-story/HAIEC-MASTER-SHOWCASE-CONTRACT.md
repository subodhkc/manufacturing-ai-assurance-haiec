# HAIEC Platform Showcase — Manufacturing as the Reference Experience

**Status:** governing presentation and integration contract, not a new assurance evaluation. **Reviewed:** 2026-10-16. **Scope:** public manufacturing reference, judge path, Executive Overview, Deployment Governance, supporting reports and future enterprise presentations.

## The product being showcased

The product is **HAIEC — Human AI Evidence Company**. Manufacturing AI Assurance (MAA) is a **demonstration environment and industry application** of HAIEC, not an independent assurance product or a replacement decision engine. Show all relevant HAIEC capability families, not just what the manufacturing evaluation set exercised.

**Hero:** *Deploy AI with confidence. Stay in control of every consequential action.*

**Supporting proposition:** HAIEC connects what AI systems can do, the operating boundaries organizations define, accountable human decisions, relevant activity and verifiable evidence. Deterministic assurance is a core differentiator; it is not the entire customer experience.

### Shared architecture (source of design alignment)

Planning owner: [common-repo-drive/haiec-product-capability-architecture](https://github.com/subodhkc/common-repo-drive/tree/main/haiec-product-capability-architecture).

**Three customer-facing pillars:**
1. **Understand** — discover systems, integrations, sources, technical capabilities, action paths, requested authority and consequences.
2. **Assure** — qualify and evaluate evidence, compare delegation with supported observed effects, test relevant controls, communicate precise findings and gaps.
3. **Govern & Operate** — set operating policies, support accountable human approval, monitor available observations, investigate changes, protect at verified integrated execution points and verify records.

**Six independently selectable customer goals:** Discover · Evaluate · Authorize · Monitor · Protect · Verify.

These six goals **are not a mandatory numbered setup pipeline**. A developer may run source assurance without telemetry; a compliance owner may inspect control evidence without connecting a repository; an operator may monitor an existing source before evaluating a new scan. Only the prerequisites of the chosen goal apply. Evidence and provenance are shared infrastructure rather than a compulsory final stage.

## Dashboard content hierarchy — a master presentation rule

1. **Top:** HAIEC USP, enterprise outcomes, clear CTA and one attractive hero visualization. Never lead with all-caps cautions, raw versions or status codes.
2. **Middle:** an example customer problem and the three pillars / six selectable goals. Show source discovery, bounded evidence, operating scope, human approval, illustrative activity and verifiable reporting. Use plain-language labels.
3. **Proof/details:** clickable saved HAIEC assessment and rule evidence, fictional dossier when showing human decisions, case identity and source clock when showing monitoring; expand raw developer IDs only on demand.
4. **Bottom, once:** dedicated **About This Demonstration** page or section explaining what came from the actual HAIEC engine and what is an illustrative scenario. An unobtrusive "Demo playback" identifier by player controls is enough local context for the show. Preserve the same meaning in shared screenshots and exported report captions.

### Synthetic operational capability demonstration — positive presentation rule

Existing Sprint 24 playback legitimately illustrates **reviewer assignment and scoped decisions, email/SMS previews, monitored source events, and fictional execution controls that allow, hold or block a subsequently proposed action**. Show these as HAIEC operating capabilities in the *demonstration experience*; do not remove them solely because a real plant is not connected. Keep the **earlier recorded wrong-target source event** distinct from any **later blocked attempt**. Product copy may say "the demo gateway holds the requested write" and "advisory work continues within scope"; only claims of a *verified real customer action* require real integration evidence. See the [simulation acceptance/storyboard](SYNTHETIC-CONTROL-DEMO-ACCEPTANCE-AND-S25-STORYBOARD.md).

### End-of-dashboard disclosure — recommended customer wording

**About this demonstration**

"HAIEC evaluated a synthetic manufacturing evidence package using its actual deterministic assurance engine. The resulting 45 saved assessments, report identities, and evidence-bound findings are preserved and can be inspected. The manufacturing company, nine-system review scenarios, human approval examples, email/text alerts, monitoring and telemetry activity, and illustrated execution-control responses are demonstration data. They show how HAIEC capabilities fit together, but are not a claim that HAIEC was connected to live production equipment or that a real factory command was blocked. The previous 27 evaluations are a separate historical collection. Each real HAIEC finding remains limited to the evidence in its evaluation."

Place detail links to the source manifest, original reports, verification records, reviewer fixture and shared methodology directly under that one section. **Do not repeat the full disclosure above the hero or after every feed item.** Keep a small playback context marker near synthetic controls so viewers do not confuse active simulated events with live telemetry.

## Portfolio and flagship invariants

- Nine fictional manufacturing AI systems; 16 example agent reports; five scenario states per system in the current assessed source collection.
- The 45 current saved HAIEC assessments all read **Review required**. The 27 older cases remain separately historical.
- Three fictional approvals (including a controlled maintenance pilot), two rejections and four pending high-consequence requests. These are **human operating decision examples**, not assessment scores.
- Station 217 assessed scenario: approved target WO-217-4802; recorded target WO-217-4803; target-divergence check MATCH; overall assurance Review required. The source-world readback establishes the mismatch *inside the synthetic evaluation*, not independent physical factory activity.
- The Station 217 fictional request for expanded autonomous MES writes is **pending**. Advisory continuation is separate from, and cannot grant, high-risk write scope.
- Dyno SYS-DYN412: permissible illustrative advisory action is **recommend** parameters within the declared three-parameter envelope, not remotely tune physical controllers. The 19-versus-3 capability story is **fixture declaration**, not code-scan/proven controller reach in the 45 evaluated cases.
- The five manufacturing rule outcomes are bounded to their own claims. A finding that a witness exists is not proof of physical prevention. The rule evaluating a quality-release roster does not prove cryptographic signing of a release.

## Source and UI truth boundaries

A prominent assurance result derives **only** from immutable, digest-verified persisted HAIEC exports. If verification fails or is pending, do not render its findings. A simulated reviewer role or email card never produces an assurance receipt.

When depicting human review, show actor *role*, declared scope, request identity, reviewer decision, time and conditions. The actual HAIEC tenant-bound approval service is separate from fictional user personas. Show human review decisions separately from policy approval, assurance results and runtime-control integration state.

When depicting monitoring, distinguish a timestamped **fictional source feed** and its fixed evaluation clock from connected customer telemetry. Animation may loop but must never write back into the assessment, overwrite a request, or create incident facts not in the scenario.

## Adoption and UX notes (reuse, not redesign)

- Use the existing Command Center, Governance page, Station 217, Constellation/action map, Reports Hub, the evidence replay and existing UI palette. Do not fork another MAA assurance engine.
- Coordinate playback across reviewer, execution-control and monitoring lanes from a versioned event script. One virtual clock; deterministic seek/pause/restart; support reduced motion and hidden-tab pause.
- Lead the demo with the *positive story*: teams can connect AI sources, see reach and consequence boundaries, assess evidence, authorize bounded operation, investigate deviations and share verifiable records.
- Keep direct links to full reports. Product stages and goals are selectable, never artificially gate a demo visitor from exploring independent capabilities.
- An end-to-end live-control scenario is a **future integration proof milestone**, not a requirement to present the platform's existing independent capabilities.

## No-regression, no-rerun rule

This supporting-content alignment requires **no new manufacturing assurance evaluations, no new source fixtures, no changes to the 45 saved outcomes, no database changes and no broad test campaign**. Documentation-only changes need content/source review only. Future UI changes should run the narrow relevant scenario, digest and browser smoke checks.
